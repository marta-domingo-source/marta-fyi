Currently

At Miro I lead design for core product experience — collaboration used by more than 100 million people. Most of my attention goes to AI-native product design: deciding which standards turn generative output into something worth shipping, and building the infrastructure that lets the rest of the organisation work that way.

I prototype in code, because the interesting part of a design is usually the part a mock hides. A technical background shows up as care rather than speed: knowing how something is built changes what you ask for, and which details quietly decide whether a result feels considered or approximate.

I still build what I design, and I’d protect that part of the job before any other.

Experience

Product design, then the systems around it, then the teams. Mostly HP, now Miro, with teaching alongside for much of it.

  • 2025 → Present
    Design Manager · Miro
    Leading design for core product experience — collaboration used by more than 100 million people. Most of it is AI-native product design: the standards and rules that turn generative output into production-ready workflows rather than a convincing demo. Alongside that, the design infrastructure that lets AI work across the organisation — systems, tooling, evaluation frameworks and rapid prototyping environments — and the review and governance practices that hold product-wide design quality.
  • 2017 → 2025
    Head of Advanced Design · HP
    Eight years and four roles, from designing products to running the Design Lab — connecting early exploration to product strategy so that go/no-go decisions rested on validated signals instead of arriving late. As Design Systems Lead before that, I moved the organisation from product-focused design teams to design-at-scale operations: a ~40% increase in reuse, and update cycles cut by ~25%. Earlier, leading product design across web, mobile and device, I unified an ecosystem of more than ten applications serving ~36K companies behind shared platform principles and decision frameworks.
